THE DOMINANCE RATING
Our answer to WAR — the ten most dominant single fantasy seasons on record. The DOM score blends three ingredients, each normalized 0–100 across every qualifying player-season and weighted: Active Points (50%) — points actually delivered to a starting lineup; Value Over Replacement (35%) — season points above a streamer at the same position; Availability (15%) — share of regular-season weeks started, the games-missed penalty. The Hi Wk column shows each player's single biggest week that season. QB/RB/WR/TE only, min. 7 weeks started. (2009–2026, regular season.)
